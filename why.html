Why players use azeroth.wiki

One plan across every tool

Talents, Legacy, equipment, professions, materials and quests read the same character plan. A plan made in one tool is already there in the next, and a share link carries all of it.

Your real characters

With World Recorder, your profile shows your characters as the game does, and each plan can follow its character's level, equipment, professions, quests, talents or Legacy. How it works.

Builds and guides from players

Players publish builds that others like and copy into their own plans, alongside guides and news for WoW Forever.

Open the tools
