Your characters on your wowdb.wiki profile

Once World Recorder is installed and your computer is linked to your account, your profile opens with your characters, each shown as the game's character sheet: class, race, level, guild, talent trees, Legacy perks, professions with their ranks and the equipment you wear.

Plans linked to a real character

Each character opens the talent calculator, Legacy planner, equipment list, professions and quests with its plan. In the tools, the Game character control links a plan to a character, and its options choose what comes from the game: level, equipment, professions and completed quests by default, talents and Legacy when you want your plan to show what the character has learned.

Public or private

Characters appear on your public profile until you clear their Public box. Money and experience are shown only to you.

When it updates

The game writes addon data when you log out, exit or reload the interface, and the World Recorder uploader sends it within seconds.
