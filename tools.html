WoW Forever tools on wowdb.wiki

Every tool shares one plan: the character you plan in the talent calculator is the same one the Legacy planner, the equipment list and the professions read. A share link carries the whole plan; signing in keeps it on every device.

Talent calculator

Every class tree with its ranks, row requirements and the level each point needs, including the earlier first point that the Legacy perk Talented brings.

Legacy planner

Separate Legacy perks for a main and an alt, with the points both share and the class talents each perk hands over.

Spellbook

Abilities by class, school and level, with every rank and the talent a rank requires.

Races

The racial abilities of up to three races side by side for one class, and the classes each race can play.

Professions

Every recipe of a profession in skill-up order, with the skill at which it turns yellow and grey.

Materials

Chosen recipes combined into one shopping list of ingredients.

Items

Items with their crafting uses, recipes and dungeon sources.

Equipment

Equipment by slot with its item bonuses and its dungeon and crafting sources.

Dungeon loot

Dungeon and raid loot boss by boss, with the dungeon, boss and item behind every drop.

Camping

Camping benefits with the skills and ingredients each one needs.

Quests

Quest givers, where they stand and the quests they begin or finish, with rewards and level and faction requirements.

World map

Continent maps with towns, flight paths and the herb and ore nodes players have gathered.

AddOns

Addons for WoW Forever found by goal, game version, required library and recency.
