The WoW Forever database and player tools

azeroth.wiki covers WoW Forever: talents and Legacy, every class's spells, races, professions and recipes, items and where they drop, dungeon bosses, quests and the world map.

Open the tools World Recorder addon

Tools

Talent calculatorEvery class tree with its ranks, row requirements and the level each point needs, including the earlier first point that the Legacy perk Talented brings.Legacy plannerSeparate Legacy perks for a main and an alt, with the points both share and the class talents each perk hands over.SpellbookAbilities by class, school and level, with every rank and the talent a rank requires.RacesThe racial abilities of up to three races side by side for one class, and the classes each race can play.ProfessionsEvery recipe of a profession in skill-up order, with the skill at which it turns yellow and grey.MaterialsChosen recipes combined into one shopping list of ingredients.ItemsItems with their crafting uses, recipes and dungeon sources.EquipmentEquipment by slot with its item bonuses and its dungeon and crafting sources.Dungeon lootDungeon and raid loot boss by boss, with the dungeon, boss and item behind every drop.CampingCamping benefits with the skills and ingredients each one needs.QuestsQuest givers, where they stand and the quests they begin or finish, with rewards and level and faction requirements.World mapContinent maps with towns, flight paths and the herb and ore nodes players have gathered.AddOnsAddons for WoW Forever found by goal, game version, required library and recency.

Your characters, from the game

With the World Recorder addon, your azeroth.wiki profile shows each of your characters as the game's character sheet: level, equipment, talents, Legacy and professions. Plans in every tool link to a real character and take from the game what you choose. How characters and plans work.

Search from your browser

The azeroth.wiki browser extension searches WoW Forever from the address bar: type wow and a name.

Builds from players

Players publish their talent and equipment builds on azeroth.wiki builds, where others like them and copy one into their own plan.
